# Volunteer Expectations

What we expect from volunteers and what volunteers can expect from Better.sg.

## Our Promise to You

As a **volunteer-run charity** with 2,000+ members, Better.sg exists because people like you dedicate their time and skills to making technology a force for good. We take that commitment seriously.

---

## What You Can Expect From Us

### Meaningful Impact 🎯

**We promise:**
- Real projects addressing actual social issues
- Direct connection to non-profits and communities we serve
- Transparency about project goals and impact metrics
- Regular updates on how your work creates change

**You'll see:**
- Users directly benefiting from your contributions
- Non-profits amplifying their impact with your tools
- Communities being empowered through technology
- Success stories and testimonials

### Respect for Your Time ⏰

**We promise:**
- No expectation of 24/7 availability
- Flexible contribution schedules
- Async-first communication
- Clear project scopes and timelines
- Understanding when life gets busy

**You won't:**
- Be pressured to attend every meeting
- Face guilt for taking breaks
- Need to explain personal commitments
- Be expected to work beyond your capacity

### Quality Support & Resources 🛠️

**We provide:**
- Well-documented codebases and READMEs
- Clear contribution guidelines
- Responsive project leads and mentors
- Technical infrastructure (repos, hosting, tools)
- Regular community events and learning opportunities

**You'll have access to:**
- Mentorship from experienced contributors
- Tech talks and workshops (AI/ML, design, etc.)
- Networking with 2,000+ like-minded individuals
- Career development opportunities
- Showcase platform for your work

### Inclusive & Safe Community 💚

**We promise:**
- Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination
- Welcoming environment for all skill levels
- Patient and kind responses to questions
- Recognition that diversity makes us stronger
- Safe spaces to make mistakes and learn

**Everyone is welcome:**
- Beginners and experts
- Students and professionals
- All backgrounds, identities, and experiences
- Technical and non-technical contributors
- Part-time and full-time volunteers

### Recognition & Growth 🌟

**We offer:**
- Public recognition for your contributions
- Opportunity to lead projects and initiatives
- Portfolio-worthy work for your career
- References and recommendations
- Networking with industry professionals and organizations

**You can:**
- Showcase projects in your portfolio
- Present at Better Socials and Beacon conference
- Build leadership experience
- Develop new technical skills
- Connect with hiring partners and recruiters

## What We Expect From You

### Commitment to Mission 🌟

**We expect:**
- Genuine interest in tech-for-good and social impact
- Alignment with Better.sg values and principles
- Desire to help communities and non-profits
- Understanding that we serve vulnerable populations

**This means:**
- Putting user needs first, especially marginalized communities
- Building with empathy and accessibility in mind
- Respecting the dignity of people we serve
- Staying mission-focused in technical decisions

### Quality Work 💯

**We expect:**
- Code that meets our engineering standards
- Tests for critical functionality
- Clear documentation for your contributions
- Timely response to code review feedback
- Willingness to iterate and improve

**This doesn't mean:**
- Perfection on first attempt
- Working long hours
- Knowing everything before you start
- Never making mistakes

### Professional Communication 💬

**We expect:**
- Respectful and kind interactions with all volunteers
- Clear communication about your availability
- Updating team if you need to step back from work
- Constructive feedback during code reviews
- Asking for help when you need it

**This looks like:**
- Responding to Slack messages within a reasonable timeframe (days, not hours)
- Updating GitHub issues you're working on
- Letting project lead know if you can't complete assigned work
- Being transparent about constraints and blockers
- Treating everyone with respect and kindness

### Collaborative Spirit 🤝

**We expect:**
- Willingness to help other volunteers
- Openness to feedback and different perspectives
- Sharing knowledge and learnings
- Contributing to team culture positively
- Supporting new contributors

**This means:**
- Reviewing PRs when you can
- Answering questions in Slack
- Sharing helpful resources and documentation
- Celebrating others' contributions
- Being patient with less experienced volunteers

### Accountability & Ownership 📝

**We expect:**
- Following through on commitments when possible
- Communicating if you can't complete something
- Taking responsibility for your work
- Being proactive about blockers
- Unassigning yourself from stale issues

**This means:**
- If you claim an issue, try to make progress on it
- If life gets busy, let the team know
- If you're blocked, ask for help
- If you can't finish, hand it off properly
- Own your mistakes and learn from them

### Continuous Learning 📚

**We expect:**
- Willingness to learn new technologies
- Openness to feedback and growth
- Asking questions when unclear
- Staying curious and engaged
- Sharing what you learn with others

**You'll grow by:**
- Working on real-world projects
- Getting code review from experienced developers
- Attending workshops and tech talks
- Mentoring newer volunteers
- Experimenting with new tools and approaches

## Commitment Levels

We understand volunteers have different availability. Choose what works for you:

### Occasional Contributor 🌿

**Time commitment:** 2-5 hours/month

**Great for:**
- Fixing small bugs
- Documentation improvements
- Attending community events
- Providing feedback and testing

**What we expect:**
- Small, focused contributions when you have time
- Communication if you claim an issue

### Regular Contributor 🌱

**Time commitment:** 5-10 hours/month

**Great for:**
- Building features
- Code reviews
- Mentoring newer volunteers
- Contributing consistently to a project

**What we expect:**
- Regular engagement with your project
- Participate in team discussions
- Help unblock other contributors

### Core Contributor 🌳

**Time commitment:** 10-20 hours/month

**Great for:**
- Project leadership
- Architecture decisions
- Mentoring and community building
- Cross-project coordination

**What we expect:**
- Consistent availability for your project
- Guide project direction and priorities
- Mentor other volunteers
- Represent project in community

### Project Lead 🌲

**Time commitment:** 15-25 hours/month

**Great for:**
- Leading a project end-to-end
- Stakeholder management with non-profits
- Team coordination and planning
- Setting technical direction

**What we expect:**
- Regular check-ins with team and stakeholders
- Manage project roadmap and priorities
- Unblock team members
- Ensure project health and sustainability

**Important:** These are guidelines, not requirements. Your commitment can flex based on your life circumstances.

## When Things Don't Go As Planned

### Life Happens 🌈

We understand that:
- Jobs get busy
- Exams come up
- Family needs attention
- Health issues arise
- Burnout happens

**What to do:**
1. Let your project lead know (no details needed)
2. Unassign yourself from issues you can't complete
3. Take the time you need
4. Come back when you're ready

**No guilt, no questions asked.**

### Changing Your Mind 🔄

It's okay to:
- Realize a project isn't the right fit
- Want to switch to a different project
- Reduce your involvement
- Take an extended break
- Step away permanently

**What to do:**
- Communicate your decision
- Hand off any active work
- No explanation required beyond "I need to step back"

### Conflicts or Issues ⚠️

If you experience:
- Harassment or discrimination
- Toxic behavior from another volunteer
- Feeling overwhelmed or unsupported
- Disagreements that can't be resolved

**What to do:**
1. Talk to your project lead if comfortable
2. Reach out to community managers
3. Email leadership directly
4. We take these issues seriously

## Getting Started

### Your First Week 👋

**Week 1 checklist:**
- [ ] Join Better.sg Slack
- [ ] Introduce yourself in #general
- [ ] Browse current projects
- [ ] Attend a Better Social event (optional)
- [ ] Pick a "good first issue" to work on

### Your First Contribution 🎉

**Steps:**
1. Find a project that interests you
2. Read the project README and CONTRIBUTING.md
3. Set up your local development environment
4. Pick an issue tagged "good first issue"
5. Ask questions in the project Slack channel
6. Submit your first PR
7. Celebrate! 🎊

### Finding Your Place 🧭

**Explore:**
- Different projects to find your passion
- Technical and non-technical roles
- Mentorship opportunities
- Leadership positions
- Community building initiatives

**Remember:** It's okay to try different things before committing.
