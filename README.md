# Better.sg Engineering Playbook

> **Building better technology, together** 🚀

Welcome to the Better.sg Engineering Playbook — our single source of truth for how we build, ship, and maintain technology at Better.sg.

> **🎯 A Note to Our Volunteers**
>
> This playbook is a **collection of best practices and guidelines**, not a rigid rulebook. We understand that volunteers contribute on their own time and with varying levels of experience. These practices exist to help us build better technology together, but we recognize that **perfect is the enemy of done**.
>
> - **Security and data protection** matters for our users, so please take those sections seriously
> - **Code quality and testing** help everyone maintain and extend the codebase
> - Everything else is **aspirational** — if you can follow it, great! If not, we'd rather have your contribution than perfect code
>
> Questions? No idea what something means? **Ask!** We're here to help, not judge.
>
> — The Better.sg Tech Team

## 🎯 Purpose

This playbook exists to:
- **Empower volunteers** with clear, actionable engineering guidelines
- **Maintain quality** without sacrificing speed or experimentation  
- **Keep our systems secure** and our data protected
- **Create consistency** across diverse projects and skill levels

## 👋 Who This Is For

- New volunteers joining Better.sg engineering teams
- Existing contributors looking for best practices
- Project leads designing new initiatives
- Anyone curious about how we work

## 🎯 Engineering Principles (TL;DR)

These are our North Stars:

### 1. **Security First** 🔐
Protect user data and privacy always. No exceptions.

### 2. **Quality Over Speed**
Ship working code, improve iteratively. Don't let perfect be the enemy of good.

### 3. **Document as You Go** 📚
Your future self (and teammates) will thank you.

### 4. **Review Everything** 👀
Two sets of eyes catch more bugs and spread knowledge.

### 5. **Fail Fast, Learn Faster** 🚀
Experiment boldly, recover quickly.

### 6. **Volunteer-Friendly** 🤝
Async-first, inclusive, welcoming. We value your time.
